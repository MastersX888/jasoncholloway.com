## 4 · Xiaohongshu (小红书) excerpt cards — three, post-translation

### Card 1 — German farmhouse / Rudolf

**Overlay (CN):** 我以为是关节炎。
**Overlay (EN):** I thought it was the arthritis.

**Visual:** stone-walled living room, woodstove, Black Forest in the window.
No cathedral stock photo — the point is a farmhouse.

**Caption (EN, verbatim excerpt):**

```
Rural Germany. A farmhouse outside Freiburg...

Rudolf Werner, 71, retired carpenter, whose hands had built furniture for forty years, opened his eyes after fifteen minutes and held his hands out in front of him and said: "My hands are vibrating. They have been vibrating for years. I thought it was the arthritis."

It was not the arthritis. Or rather: it was also the arthritis, and it was also the frequency, and the distinction between a pathology and a perception depended entirely on whether you believed the body was a machine or an instrument.

— Masters X: The Kingdom
```

**Caption (CN draft, for post-translation typesetting):**

```
德国乡下。弗莱堡外的农舍。

七十一岁的退休木匠 Rudolf Werner 睁开眼，把手伸到面前，说：「我的手在振。振了很多年。我以为是关节炎。」

不是关节炎。或者说：也是关节炎，也是频率。病理和感知的分界，取决于你相信身体是机器，还是乐器。

——《国度》（频率三部曲 III）
```
