## Description (paste into YouTube Studio)

Umberto Eco's *Foucault's Pendulum* changed what literary conspiracy fiction could be — real history as engine, paranoia as structure, manuscripts as technology. Thirty-five years later, readers still search for books that hit the same nerve.

In this video I map the **Eco → Brown → Kostova** comp trajectory, explain what each tradition does differently, and introduce **Masters X** as a series that bridges Central European codex history with Anglo-American thriller pacing — with free Field Notes separating fact from fiction.
