## 2. Why three, not four or eight

Two facts moved the plan off the Pass 2 prompt's 4-vol/8-vol starting points:

**(a) The source counts were wrong in the baseline report.** The print master's own section
headers — verified by a full parse of the docx (115,504 entries exactly) — give a completely
different letter distribution than the Letter A report: there is **no 700-page Letter E**
(E = 3,907 entries, ~69 pp). The largest letter is **S (12,286 entries, 217 pp)**, which splits
nothing. Measured letter ledger (entries → pages as typeset):

A 6,132→108 · B 8,537→150 · C 9,214→163 · D 6,627→118 · E 3,907→69 · F 4,542→81 ·
G 4,999→88 · H 5,624→99 · I 2,843→52 · J 1,383→26 · K 3,742→67 · L 4,900→86 ·
M 8,397→147 · N 2,570→45 · O 2,488→45 · P 7,129→126 · Q 395→8 · R 6,194→110 ·
S 12,286→217 · T 4,876→87 · U 1,606→30 · V 2,091→38 · W 3,541→63 · X 60→3 ·
Y 609→12 · Z 812→16

**(b) Production typography beat the density baseline.** The Cursor sample measured 36.6
entries/page in Helvetica/Georgia. The production setting (EB Garamond SB headwords, compact
band-tag key, guide-word running heads) measures **56.8 entries/page** on pure entry pages —
readable at 9/7.6 pt, verified in proof renders. Total set drops from the projected ~3,156
pages to a measured **2,062**.

Three volumes means: one slipcase die, three cover wraps, three ISBNs plus the set ISBN,
the lowest unit cost per set, and volumes that shelve as siblings (1.3–1.8 in spines).

## 3. Alternate splits (documented, not recommended)

- **4 volumes** (~515 avg): A–C 421 / D–H 455 / I–P 554 / Q–Z 594 (+ front/back matter).
  Works, but adds a SKU and thins the spines without a readability gain.
- **8 volumes @ ~260 pp**: rejected — spine widths fall under 0.55 in, spine type suffers,
  slipcase cost rises, and the set price climbs for no reader benefit.

## 4. Volume contents

- **Every volume:** half title · volume title (number + range + heptagram) · copyright with
  set + volume ISBN slots · How to Read an Entry (specimen + compact tag key + Companion
  pointer). Entries begin recto.
- **Vol. 1 additionally:** the print master's epigraphs (Tesla; John 1:1) and full Preface,
  closing with the science/fiction seam note (Rev. 3 §7) in its first spread.
- **Vol. 3 additionally (back matter, from the print master):** Prayers & Sacred Words (28
  prayers with composite CRFs) · Healing Words · Sigil Research · Symbology Research ·
  Appendices A–G (including the ARPAbet key at Appendix F, which every volume's entry-key
  page cross-references).
