### Judgment calls

- **"building", not "apartment", in three structural sentences** (4276–4278, 4367). In those lines the noun is the load-bearing carcass, not the home: the tremor conducts out of the screwdriver *into* it, and it is the thing that "had limestone foundations that resonated at 109 Hz." Line 4367 already paired the two nouns in the original — "as if his body and the building were synchronizing" — so "the building's own limestone foundation" is the reading the sentence was already carrying. Everywhere the noun means the home or the home's acoustics, it is "apartment," including "the apartment hummed," "the sound of the apartment," and "listen to the apartment."
- **"home" in the aphorism at 3663.** "You learn the rhythm of your wife the way you learn the rhythm of your house" is a universal second-person maxim, not a reference to Blake's address; "your home" keeps it universal and removes the house signal without over-specifying a proverb.
- **The grandfather stays the builder** (4217, and 92 which was already "the structure"). A grandfather who built the building the family has occupied for three generations is consistent with the ruling, so nothing was demolished to accommodate it.
- **Anaphora preserved.** Where the original repeated the noun for rhythm — "The house was clean. The house was empty," "The house was quiet. The house was tired. The house kept standing," "a house that had been her house and was her house again" — the replacement repeats too.
- **Line wrapping untouched.** Replacements run 4 characters long, so a few lines now exceed the extract's ~75-column wrap. The corpus is an audit artifact, not the typeset source, so no reflow was performed.
