# COMPANION SPINE CALCULATION — 8.5 × 11 case bound
## Pass 2 · assumes 50# white B&W bulk (512 PPI per Ingram paper spec)

**Verify at RFQ:** BookVault's 80 gsm/50 lb equivalent bulk may differ slightly; their cover
template is authoritative for the case spine. Numbers below drive the art proofs.

| SKU | Pages | Text block (pp ÷ 512) | Case spine (block + 0.25 in allowance) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Companion** | 358 | 0.699 in | **0.949 in** |
| Dictionary Vol. 1 (A–D) | 546 | 1.066 in | **1.316 in** |
| Dictionary Vol. 2 (E–M) | 714 | 1.395 in | **1.645 in** |
| Dictionary Vol. 3 (N–Z) | 802 | 1.566 in | **1.816 in** |

**Slipcase interior stack:** 1.316 + 1.645 + 1.816 + 0.125 clearance = **4.90 in**; exterior
adds wall thickness per vendor die. Slipcase art proof carries these figures in its slug.

Crème-stock alternate (444 PPI) is **not recommended** for this program — white stock is the
working-file register and keeps Vol. 3 at 1.81 in rather than 2.06 in.

Spine copy fits comfortably at all four widths (minimum 0.95 in); keep all spine elements
≥ 0.0625 in from spine edges for wrap tolerance.
