## Narration script (verbatim)

### HOOK (0:00–0:55)

*[Plain county map; place five pins one at a time in silence; then VO.]*

> Two thousand years of people — none of whom could have read each other's books — looked at the same thirty miles of river bluff and said: this ground matters. My novel didn't invent that. It just needed a mechanism.

**OST:** `5 traditions · 30 miles · 2,000 years`

---

### BEAT 1 — Pin one: mound builders (0:55–2:45)

> Hopewell, 100 BCE to 700 CE, westernmost frontier. About thirty sites. Stone vault tombs — doorway-entered chambers inside the mounds. Missouri's cave-art record is real — Diaz-Granados and Duncan. They were already building underground.

**OST:** `They were already building underground.`

---

### BEAT 2 — Pin two: the name (2:45–4:15)

> The Osage and *Wah-kon-tah* — the sacred mystery that connects everything. Different name. Same phenomenon.

**OST:** `Different name. Same phenomenon.`

---

### BEAT 3 — Pin three: Zion (4:15–6:30)

> July 1831, Doctrine and Covenants 57. Independence as the center place. Eden in Jackson County. Adam-ondi-Ahman seventy miles north. Sixty-three acres, Temple Lot, dedicated August 3, 1831 — temple never built, maintained today by three denominations.
>
> And here is the encyclopedia's honesty rule: there is no Temple Lot scene in the trilogy. We checked. The site is gravity, not location.

**OST:** `63 acres · Aug 3, 1831 · never built`

---

### BEAT 4 — Pin four: temple built (6:30–8:30)

> Community of Christ, 1994, Gyo Obata — sanctuary spiraling one hundred ninety-five feet on a nautilus. Fibonacci as literal architectural DNA, in the prophesied Zion. Readers assume I invented this one. I did not.

**OST:** `1994 · 195 ft · Fibonacci`

---

### BEAT 5 — Pin five: engineer (8:30–10:15)

> Roger Billings — hydrogen car at seventeen; Independence in 1980 for explicitly prophetic reasons; the Church of Jesus Christ in Zion; one hundred eighty underground acres of Bethany Falls limestone, laboratories under the New Jerusalem. The William Masters parallel — conceded openly.

**OST:** `180 acres · under Zion`

---

### BEAT 6 — Stone under all five (10:15–11:15)

> Bethany Falls limestone. SubTropolis room-and-pillar dimensions. The honest gap: no published acoustics study of the KC underground. The fiction is sited, not sourced.
>
> Nadia: *"At some point, coincidence becomes evidence."* The encyclopedia's job is to tell you which side of that line every sentence is on.

---

### CTA (11:15–12:00)

> Essay Two — "The Ground Itself Is Significant" — with the full five-tradition record and every source. jasoncholloway.com.
>
> The facts are in the files. The fiction is in the books. The encyclopedia holds the seam.
