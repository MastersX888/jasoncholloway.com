## Photography Notes

**Required images (need from Jason):**
- `public/covers/book1-case.png` — Vol I case cover (jacket removed), clean scan or photo
- `public/covers/book2-case.png` — Vol II case cover
- `public/covers/book3-case.png` — Vol III case cover
- `public/covers/omnibus-case.png` — Omnibus case cover

**Photography tips for Pinterest:**
1. Dark background (matte black paper or slate)
2. Natural or warm directional lighting from upper left
3. Shoot from slightly above (10-15° angle)
4. Include the removed jacket beside the book to tell the story
5. Pinterest optimal ratio: 1000×1500 (2:3 vertical)
6. For the "side by side" pin: jacketed book on left, case-exposed on right

**Crop specs for Pinterest:**
- Standard pin: 1000×1500px
- Idea pin: 1080×1920px
- Minimum: 600×900px
