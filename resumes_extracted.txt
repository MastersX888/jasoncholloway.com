PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
Healthcare data professional with nearly a decade of experience managing clinical and public-health data, reporting pipelines, and quality-improvement initiatives in HIPAA-regulated, federally funded environments. Skilled in SAS, SQL, advanced Excel/VBA automation, Tableau, and Power BI, with a track record of building validation systems that drive measurable error reduction and of translating complex health datasets into decisions for clinical, epidemiological, and executive audiences. Deep experience with CDC performance measures, population-health analysis, and demographic stratification. Google-certified in data analytics, advanced data analytics, and business intelligence. Seeking a remote or hybrid healthcare data analyst role where analytical rigor and end-to-end data ownership improve patient and program outcomes.
CORE COMPETENCIES
Healthcare & Public-Health Data:  CDC Performance Measures • Population-Health Analysis • Demographic Stratification • Clinical & Program Reporting • HIPAA • Regulatory Compliance
Data & Analytics:  SAS • SQL • Advanced Excel & VBA Automation • Tableau • Power BI • Data Validation & Integrity • Statistical Reporting
Tools & Collaboration:  Jira • Asana • Smartsheet • MS Project • Basecamp • Technical Documentation • Data Governance • Cross-Functional Collaboration
Technical & Cloud:  Google Cloud • Web Deployment (Next.js, Cloudflare Pages) • Workflow & AI-Agent Automation
PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
Data Manager — WISEWOMAN & Show Me Healthy Women Programs
Missouri DHSS, Division of Community and Public Health  |  Remote  |  Jan 2021 – Present
Lead data management for two federally funded CDC chronic-disease and cancer-screening programs (WISEWOMAN and Show Me Healthy Women), owning the full cycle of clinical and program data collection, validation, and CDC-mandated submissions under HIPAA and strict federal reporting standards.
Built SAS-based clinical analytics for CDC Performance Measures, including blood-pressure-control analysis with demographic stratification, to evaluate population-health outcomes and support federal reporting.
Designed and deployed automated validation and error-reporting systems in advanced Excel/VBA — including deduplication logic and trend analysis — reducing submission error rates to the lowest levels on record while cutting manual review time.
Serve as primary liaison among epidemiologists, IT teams, and program staff to define data requirements and align pipeline development across clinical and technical functions.
Authored and maintain data-standards and process documentation, creating a single source of truth for distributed stakeholders and Regional Program Coordinators.
Conduct recurring data audits to proactively identify, track, and mitigate integrity risks and maintain HIPAA and regulatory compliance.
Research Data Analyst
MO Dept. of Economic Development / Dept. of Higher Education & Workforce Development  |  Jefferson City, MO  |  Oct 2017 – Jan 2021
Analyzed and validated large state and federal workforce datasets to meet recurring reporting deadlines and inform policy research.
Partnered cross-functionally to interpret complex workforce data and ensure compliance across regular reporting cycles.
