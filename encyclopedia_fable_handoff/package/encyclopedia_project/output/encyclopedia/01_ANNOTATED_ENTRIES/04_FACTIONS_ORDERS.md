## THE KEEPERS

**In the novels.** The preservationist order. "We've been operating in Kansas City since **1924**. Protecting this knowledge. Guiding those who are ready to receive it… Since Father Moreau was murdered" (*Omnibus*, Vol. I ch. 15). They maintain the West Bottoms warehouse archive of Latin, Greek, and Arabic manuscripts; they identified Blake; they trained Nadia from childhood ("They were training me to be useful to the Keepers. There's a difference"). Doctrinally non-violent: "The Keepers don't kill."

**In the world.** The Keepers are written not as a lodge but as an *institutional pattern* — which is exactly what the research file argues real knowledge-gatekeeping looks like. The documented chain runs Jesuit education → Edmund A. Walsh, S.J., founding Georgetown's School of Foreign Service on November 25, 1919 (six years before the official U.S. Foreign Service) → Walsh's own intelligence-adjacent career (Papal Relief Mission to Russia 1922; Nuremberg consultant 1945–46) → the SFS-to-intelligence-services pipeline: `research_synthesis/JESUIT_GEORGETOWN_CIA_RESEARCH.md` (headline: "they're an INSTITUTIONAL PATTERN embedded in the very fabric of elite education"). The Kansas City texture (Jesuit Rockhurst; the 1820s Jesuit vineyards) is in `JESUIT_QUICK_REFERENCE.md` and `KC_CHURCHES_RESEARCH_ANALYSIS.md`.

**Cross-references:** Volkov, Nadia · Moreau, Father · The Custodians · The Jesuit Bridge (Part Three essay) · 1924.

## THE CUSTODIANS

**In the novels.** The suppressors. "The Custodians believe humanity isn't ready" — and unlike the Keepers, they kill: "The Custodians killed your father to stop that" (*Omnibus*, Vol. I ch. 15). Implicated in James Masters' September 2010 death ("dead on a straight road in perfect weather") and, by the order's own accounting, in the 1924 murder of Father Moreau that made Kansas City contested ground.

**In the world.** The suppression ethic has direct precedent in the skeptic camp of the documented 1980s Chinese parapsychology debate — researchers denounced for "feudal sorcery," publications attacked, demands the work be stopped (`research_synthesis/COMPREHENSIVE_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md` §I.2) — and, on the Western side, in the classification reflex the same file records ("The Americans classified everything"). The murders are fiction; the institutional instinct is documented.

**Cross-references:** Masters, James · Moreau, Father · The Keepers · The Completion Sect.

## THE COMPLETION SECT & COGNIGENICS

**In the novels.** The weaponizers, operating behind a corporate face. Dr. Helena Vance's hotel-bar pitch ("Research consortium") collapses under Blake's arithmetic: "With a twenty-nine percent failure rate." "Phase III is at twelve percent" (*Omnibus*, Vol. II). Cognigenics is the Sect's commercial instrument — patent filings the Foundation's counsel tracks, and a shared ancestry the trilogy is explicit about: the same historical research is "the direct ancestor of the Foundation's work and also the direct ancestor of Cognigenics' work, and the distance between the two legacies was not in the technology but in the ethics" (*Omnibus*, Vol. II). Nadia's charter line draws the boundary: consent "distinguishes the Foundation from Cognigenics… distinguishes preparation from coercion."

**In the world.** The weaponizer camp maps to CIA translation faction three (`COMPREHENSIVE_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md` §I.2) and the Gateway Process assessment. **`Cognigenics.txt` missing from disk** (listed in inventory only) — do not cite until restored; see `output/VERIFICATION_LOG.md`.

**Cross-references:** Vance, Dr. Helena · Fifty-Two-Week Protocol · The Governments Were Studying It Too (Part Three essay).

## THE MASTERS FOUNDATION

**In the novels.** The fourth position, incorporated at a kitchen table: "The Masters Foundation for Acoustic Research. 501(c)(3). Tax-exempt. Permanent" (*Omnibus*, Vol. II) — later styled the **William Masters Foundation** on the Distribution File's title page (*DF*, p. i), an in-universe renaming/honorific evolution (`canon/CANON.md` §3). Institutional anatomy across Vols. II–III: the Quality Hill entrance and first cohort (23 candidates); the Hotel Phillips office; the Moreau chamber 160 feet down; a fellowship program running through ordinary university channels (Yuki Tanaka); Marcus Chen's teacher-training directorate; survival of a federal investigation and the Holt hearing (Dirksen 106); and, terminally, Andrew Chen's decision to give the whole archive away — a network "across fourteen countries and showed no signs of stopping."

**In the world.** The Foundation is the trilogy's ethical inversion of its own real-world model: where Roger Billings' Independence compound is fenced, surveilled, and described by former employees as cult-like (`research_synthesis/ROGER_BILLINGS_MASTERS_X_ANALYSIS.md`), the Foundation is consent-based, IRB-submitted, and finally public-domain. Its research posture mirrors the "preserver" camp's demand for open scientific verification in the Chinese debate (`COMPREHENSIVE_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md` §I.2).

**Cross-references:** Volkov, Nadia · Chen, Andrew · The Distribution File · SubTropolis & the Moreau Chamber · Fifty-Two-Week Protocol.
