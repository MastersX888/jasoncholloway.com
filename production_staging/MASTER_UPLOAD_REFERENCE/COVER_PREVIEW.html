Print cover review — SCP_UploadReady_Full_2026-07-28

Each print cover below is a full-bleed spread at 300 dpi. The page is the bleed artwork — no white template margin, no trim or fold marks, no recolour.

Page size is the bleed spread. Ingram treats the whole PDF page as the cover, so a file at template document size (24 × 12.5″ and friends) would ship its white template margin as part of the wrap. Every file here is cropped to Ingram's bleed-artwork dimensions for its current page count, and the caption under each cover states the page size to check against the template.

What changed. The covers generated earlier tonight were discarded. They had a hand-rolled RGB→CMYK conversion that lifted the black ground to a washed brown, the IngramSpark template raster (pink/blue guide zones and instruction text) composited into the artwork instead of used as a guide, and panel geometry that assumed the bleed area was centred in the document when Ingram places it off-centre.

Why the artwork itself is unchanged. Every new template Ingram issued for the rebuilt interiors specifies the same spine as the live edition — 0.500″, 0.625″, 0.563″ and 1.625″ on the hardcovers, and paperback changes of 0.018″ or less, all inside Ingram's ±0.0625″ tolerance. No cover needed redrawing.
