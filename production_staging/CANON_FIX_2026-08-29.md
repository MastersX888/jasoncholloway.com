# Canon fix — Book 1 Chapter One — 2026-08-29

Author ruling obtained and applied. Two sentences in Chapter One of
*The Inheritance of Frequency*. Companion to `EPUB_REBUILD_2026-08-29.md`
(the scene-break centering fix), which shipped in the same rebuild.

## The two corrections

**1. Grandfather's death year.**
`CANON.md` records William Masters d. 2003 and James Masters d. September 2010.
Blake's line gave the father's year for the grandfather's death.

```diff
- "My grandfather died in 2010. His estate was settled years ago."
+ "My grandfather died in 2003. His estate was settled years ago."
```

Zero character delta, so it cannot reflow or repaginate.

**2. "A grandson he would never meet."**
Contradicted ~900 words later in the same chapter, where William picks
nine-year-old Blake up at LAX in the summer of 1999 and spends three months
teaching him to fly ("there's no chaos in nature, Blake"). They met, at length.

```diff
- William had set this in motion, aimed at a future grandson he would never meet.
+ William had set this in motion, aimed at a grandson who did not yet exist.
```

Minus five characters. Verified pagination-neutral by controlled A/B (below).
