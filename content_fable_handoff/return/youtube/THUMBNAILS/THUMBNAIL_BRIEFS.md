# Thumbnail Briefs — EP01–EP05

**Global rules (from THUMBNAILS.md, unchanged):** 1280×720 · JPG/PNG <2 MB · black field ~90% of frame · one hero object, center-left · gold Cinzel headline, THREE WORDS MAX · series footer `f = 111.2 Hz` small, bottom-right · no screaming faces · no AI-generated imagery · readable at 25% zoom.

Workflow per episode: shoot the still during the hero take → Figma 1280×720 → type per template → export → 25% zoom check → fewer words if unreadable.
