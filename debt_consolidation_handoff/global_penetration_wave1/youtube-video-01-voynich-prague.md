## Description (paste into YouTube Studio)

The Voynich Manuscript is the most famous undeciphered book in the world — held at Yale's Beinecke Library, linked to Rudolf II's court in Prague, and still unread after six centuries.

In this video I trace why the Voynich sits at the center of a real-history puzzle that stretches from Bohemia to modern conspiracy fiction — and why Prague keeps appearing in the manuscript's orbit.

I'm Jason Carroll Holloway, author of the Masters X trilogy (Seventh City Press) — a literary conspiracy thriller for readers of Umberto Eco's Foucault's Pendulum, Dan Brown, and Elizabeth Kostova's The Historian. The trilogy's manuscript system is **fiction**; the history in this video is real research from my Field Notes.
