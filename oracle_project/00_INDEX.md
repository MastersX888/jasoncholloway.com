## Premise (one sentence)

After the frequency becomes public fact, Blake discovers that every path he walked was already being recorded — and that the Oracle who inks his footprints is the woman who left him, returned to him, and has always been listening on a different band.

---

## Design Mandate

- **Advanced thriller** with Eco/Kostova gravity, not airport-puzzle velocity.
- **Intellectual** — every revelation grounded in visit-able places or cite-able research.
- **Emotional** — a recognition story: two people who complete each other's perception without sharing the same wavelength. Never say "twin flames."
- **Architecture** — structure is theme. Cycles, oracles, and time are not decoration; they are the plot machine.
