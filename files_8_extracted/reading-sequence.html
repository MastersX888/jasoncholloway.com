The The Reading Sequence

The reading list is not devotional. Each text is assigned because it contains a specific technical element the candidate will later need: a proportion, a posture, a discipline of attention, a fragment of the coupling model.

Within the Masters X Trilogy, the William Masters Foundation prescribes a fixed sequence of fifteen texts as the first phase of preparation — the reading sequence held in the Strahov and Charles University collections. The Ars Notoria is read first, in ignorance, and last, in understanding. Between those two readings lie twenty-one texts that make the difference. What follows is the annotated sequence as it appears in the distribution file Andrew Chen released to the public domain.

The Sequence
  1. 01

    Sirach (Ecclesiasticus)

    Ben Sira (c. 180 BCE) · Hebrew/Greek · Strahov Premonstratensian Library

    Fifty-one chapters plus an implied fifty-second silence. The template for the 52-week protocol.

  2. 02

    2 Esdras (Fourth Book of Ezra)

    Anonymous (1st c. CE) · Latin · Charles University Archives

    The text that broke everything open. The apocalyptic vision read as preparation.

  3. 03

    The Gospel of Mary

    Anonymous (2nd c.) · Coptic · Berlin Codex 8502.1

    The ascent of the soul through the four powers. Peter's dismissal is the institutional failure the Foundation was built to avoid.

  4. 04

    The Ars Notoria

    Anonymous (13th c.) · Latin · Strahov DG.IV.7

    The core text. The notae as acoustic-geometric specifications.

  5. 05

    Sefer Yetzirah

    Anonymous (attrib. Abraham) · Hebrew · Charles University MS 1892

    The book of formation. The thirty-two paths and the doctrine of sounded letters.

  6. 06

    The Cloud of Unknowing

    Anonymous (14th c.) · Middle English · Charles University MS 3310

    The apophatic discipline. How not-knowing prepares the listening mind.

  7. 07

    De Harmonia Mundi

    Francesco Giorgi (1525) · Latin · Strahov BF.II.14

    Cathedral proportion as sounded ratio. The numerical soul of architecture.

  8. 08

    The Zohar (Sifra di-Tzeniuta)

    Anonymous (13th c.) · Aramaic · Strahov DG.IV.19

    The book of concealment. The face and the counter-face.

  9. 09

    Harmonices Mundi (Bk III–IV)

    Johannes Kepler (1619) · Latin · Charles University MS 2890

    The consonances as geometric necessity. The ear as an instrument of proof.

  10. 10

    The Chymical Wedding

    J.V. Andreae (1616) · German · Strahov BF.III.1

    The seven-day preparation. The allegory read as protocol.

  11. 11

    Theologia Germanica

    Anonymous (14th c.) · German · Strahov BF.IV.8

    The surrendered will. The emptying that precedes reception.

  12. 12

    The Emerald Tablet (with glosses)

    Anonymous (attrib. Hermes) · Latin · Charles University MS 1901

    As above, so below. The coupling principle in its oldest formulation.

  13. 13

    Moreau’s Chamber Journals (I–III)

    Father Benjamin Moreau (1870s) · French/English · William Masters Foundation Archive

    The original SubTropolis chamber construction. The Specchi mirrors installed.

  14. 14

    The Specchi Production Records

    Specchi family (1340–1720) · Italian/Latin · Venetian State Archives / Eva Černá transcriptions

    Three hundred eighty years of parabolic acoustic mirrors. Speculorum perfectum.

  15. 15

    Andrew Chen — Reconstruction Notes

    A. Chen (present day) · English · William Masters Foundation

    The bridge document. How the preceding texts were read into a protocol.
