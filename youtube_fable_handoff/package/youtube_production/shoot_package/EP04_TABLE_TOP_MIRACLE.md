### BEAT 1 — Chladni's plates (0:50–2:30)

> Ernst Chladni showed the world that sound organizes matter. Draw a bow along a metal plate edge, sprinkle sand, and the sand migrates to the nodal lines — the places the plate is not moving. Change the frequency, change the geometry. Not metaphor. Measurement.
>
> Grade this one honestly: *measured*. You can reproduce it on your kitchen table tonight.

**OST:** `frequency → figure`

---

### BEAT 2 — Jenny's morphology (2:30–4:45)

> A century and a half later, Hans Jenny systematized it. Crystal oscillators instead of violin bows. Liquids instead of sand. His triad is still the clearest statement: the wave you hear, the pattern you see, the periodicity you feel — one phenomenon, three registers.
>
> The demonstration that matters for fiction: turn the tone off, the pattern collapses. Turn it back on, the *same* pattern returns. Order is not permanent. It is conditional on frequency.
>
> Jenny's line: "One can hear what one sees."

**OST:** `"One can hear what one sees."`

*[If possible: film tone-off / tone-on on plate for cutaway.]*

---

### BEAT 3 — Medieval hypothesis (4:45–6:45)

> The trilogy asks a question historians have not run in a lab: what if sustained chant in resonant stone, over water basins, over years, produced patterns visible enough to copy? The Ars Notoria's *notae* are real figures — medieval manuals for accelerated learning. Scholars like Claire Fanger and Julien Véronèse read them as functional cognitive technology, not sorcery.
>
> Here is the seam, stated to camera: *That the notae ARE cymatics patterns — frozen sound — is my claim. No historian makes it. It's the novel's one experiment the lab hasn't run.*

**OST:** `measured / scholarly / invented`

---

### BEAT 4 — Malta, honestly (6:45–8:30)

> The 110 Hz literature matters — Cook, Pajot, Leuchter, 2008; small sample; prefrontal shift reported at resonance. The Hypogeum has other published resonances too — 68, 70, 114. I did not cite Malta to prove Blake Masters. I cited it to show where 111.2 sits: one decimal past the measured stone, a signature, not a citation.

**OST:** `110 Hz — measured · 111.2 Hz — mine`

---

### BEAT 5 — Water (8:30–9:30)

> Jenny filmed liquids. The trilogy keeps returning to water — Aldric's basin, Moreau's river, the Ozark lake. The research note behind all of it is simple: *Water amplifies cymatics effects.* Fiction uses that as physics. Label it.
