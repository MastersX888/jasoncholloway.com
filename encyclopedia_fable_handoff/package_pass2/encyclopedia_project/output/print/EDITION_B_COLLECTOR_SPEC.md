# EDITION B — COLLECTOR ISSUE SPECIFICATION
## The Masters X Universe Encyclopedia · numbered edition of 350

## 1. What Edition B is

Same 7 × 10 interior as Edition A plus a **limitation leaf** (recto, ahead of the epigraph):
"This edition … is limited to three hundred fifty numbered copies, of which this is
Number ___ of 350," with a signature line and the heptagram. Already typeset —
`print/interior_7x10_EDITION_B_COLLECTOR.pdf`, 371 pp (submit 372). Numbering and signature
are done by hand in ink, per collector convention; do not print the numbers.

**Edition size:** 350 (set in type). Changing it means re-issuing the interior — confirm
before print. **Target retail:** $125–150 (publication plan band).

## 2. Vendor recommendation: BookVault Bespoke (primary)

Researched July 10, 2026. BookVault's Bespoke program is the strongest fit for a 250–500
run without offset capital:

- Foil stamping on matte lamination (gold — matches the brand's foil-simulating CMYK on
  Edition A; here it becomes real foil)
- Custom sprayed edges (~£6/unit for custom design; solid colors less) — **recommended:
  solid black edges**, the working-file object rendered ceremonial; a frequency-band
  pattern spray is the upgrade option
- Printed endpapers — **recommended: the heptagram construction drawing, gold line on
  black**, front and rear
- Ribbon marker (gold) + head/tail bands (black)
- Slipcases available; single-copy minimums, so numbered short runs carry no plate minimum

US alternates if landed cost or trim availability disqualifies BookVault: **Bookmobile**
(Minneapolis — short-run cloth + foil + slipcase, strong reference-book portfolio) and
**48 Hour Books** (foil setup $250–600). Get all three quotes at final page count.

## 4. Foil die note for the cover phase

The cover designer delivers Edition B art with all gold elements isolated on a single spot
layer — that layer becomes the foil die. No gradients in foil elements; minimum stroke
0.5 pt at the die.
