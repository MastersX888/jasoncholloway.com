## 2. Brand system (locked — from BRAND_SOURCE.md)

- Ground: 100 K black field.
- Gold: CMYK ≈ (0, 0.15, 0.60, 0.25) — foil-simulating build; no RGB golds.
- Display: **Cinzel**, wide-tracked caps. Secondary: **EB Garamond Italic**.
- Imprint mark: the {7/3} heptagram (line-drawn; the interior colophon and title page use
  the same construction — keep stroke geometry consistent).
- Epigraph device: "The pattern beneath everything."

## 3. Front cover copy stack (top to bottom)

1. Eyebrow: `MASTERS X` — Cinzel, tracked, gold
2. Title: `THE UNIVERSE ENCYCLOPEDIA` — Cinzel Bold, gold, dominant
3. Subtitle: *The Facts Behind the Fiction* — EB Garamond Italic
4. Author: `JASON CARROLL HOLLOWAY` — Cinzel, tracked
5. Heptagram mark

**Art direction:** sibling, not clone, of the trilogy jackets. This is the reference volume
— more restraint, more structure. A ruled/gridded treatment (frequency bands, the DF's
tabular texture, or the heptagram construction lines rendered as engineering drawing) keeps
kinship with the trilogy's black-and-gold while declaring apparatus rather than narrative.
Do not reuse trilogy hero art.

## 4. Spine

Reading top-to-bottom: `MASTERS X` (eyebrow) · `THE UNIVERSE ENCYCLOPEDIA` (dominant) ·
`HOLLOWAY` · heptagram at foot. At the as-typeset 0.72 in this fits at restrained sizes;
at projected ≥1.09 in the stack gains air. Keep all spine copy ≥0.0625 in from spine edges
(Ingram wrap tolerance).

## 5. Back cover

- Description block (≤ ~200 words; source from INGRAM_METADATA.md description, cut down)
- The two-register explainer in one line: fiction marked IN THE NOVELS, record marked
  IN THE WORLD — set the two labels in Cinzel as on the interior
- Imprint block: heptagram + `SEVENTH CITY PRESS · KANSAS CITY`
- **ISBN/barcode zone:** 2 × 1.2 in white-backed barcode, lower right, ≥0.25 in from trim;
  ISBN is the NEW assignment (placeholder 979-8-XXXXXXX-XX-X in the interior — replace both
  places together)
- Price: US $49.95 encoded in the barcode add-on (per publication plan)
