# SPINE CALCULATION
## The Masters X Universe Encyclopedia · 7 × 10 case laminate, IngramSpark

**PPI source:** IngramSpark/Lightning Source Paper Specifications, rev. 8/18/23 —
B&W 50 lb white = **512 PPI** · B&W 50 lb crème = **444 PPI** · 70 lb white = 377 PPI.

Text-block spine = page count ÷ PPI. Case laminate adds a board/binding allowance
(~+0.25 in total across two boards and crash); **the figure printed on Ingram's Cover
Template Generator output is the authoritative spine width** — regenerate the template at
the cover phase with the final page count and read the spine from it. The numbers below are
for planning the cover art's spine zone.

## Rules for the cover phase

1. **Do not** commit spine art until Pass 3 pagination is final; a 250-page swing moves the
   spine by half an inch on white stock.
2. Recommended stock stays **50 lb white** (thinner spine at length, correct working-file
   paper color; the trilogy's crème warmth is a fiction-register cue this volume should not
   borrow).
3. Spine type: Cinzel caps reading top-to-bottom, comfortably legible at ≥1.0 in; at the
   as-typeset 0.72 in keep the title stack to `MASTERS X · THE UNIVERSE ENCYCLOPEDIA ·
   HOLLOWAY` with the heptagram at the foot.
4. Ingram tolerance is ±0.0625 in on spine placement — keep spine copy inside a safety
   inset of at least that on both edges.
