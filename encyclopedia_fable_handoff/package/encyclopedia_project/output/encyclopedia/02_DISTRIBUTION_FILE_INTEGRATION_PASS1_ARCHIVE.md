# DISTRIBUTION FILE → ENCYCLOPEDIA INTEGRATION PLAN
## How the 247-page in-universe artifact maps into the reference volume

**Source:** `sources/distribution_file_fulltext.txt` (247 pp, structure per its own contents page and `universe_memory/04_STORY_CANON_DIGEST.md`)
**Governing constraint:** Publication plan targets a 480–600 pp single volume at 7×10, with an IngramSpark hardcover ceiling of 840 pp (`encyclopedia_project/01_PUBLICATION_PLAN.md` §1, §5). A full 247-page reprint inside the standard edition consumes 41–51% of the page budget and crowds out the annotations that justify the book. **Recommendation: excerpt in the standard edition; full facsimile in the collector edition only** (consistent with plan §7.5's open decision, argued below).

---

## 1. What the Distribution File is, editorially

Three properties drive the strategy:

1. **It is already designed as a document, not prose.** Running footer (f = 111.2 Hz), front-matter telemetry block, five parts with page ranges, chapter cross-keys to the trilogy ("The chapter references locate each part within the events of the Masters X Trilogy. The distribution file is the artifact those chapters describe" — *DF* contents page). It reads as an artifact; excerpting preserves that quality better than reflowing.
2. **It is in-universe CC0.** The fictional public-domain dedication ("The file cannot be un-released. That was the point," *DF* p. ii) is a gift to the encyclopedia's frame: the reference volume can "reprint" it without narrative friction, and the collector facsimile can carry the CC0 page as its own justification.
3. **Its parts map unevenly onto encyclopedia sections.** Parts I and III are reference-dense (tables, thresholds, derivations) and duplicate ground the encyclopedia's Section III entries cover analytically. Parts II and IV are the most artifact-flavored (specifications, training voice). The Appendix is raw data.

## 3. Collector edition — full facsimile

Adopt the publication plan's own artifact-model comp (*S.* / *House of Leaves*, plan §3B): the complete 247-page Distribution File as a **separate saddle-stitch or perfect-bound facsimile in the slipcase pocket**, not bound into the reference volume. Benefits: (a) the standard edition's page budget is untouched; (b) the facsimile becomes the collector edition's headline differentiator, supporting the $125–150 price point (plan §3B); (c) the DF's own page numbering, footer, and CC0 page survive intact.
