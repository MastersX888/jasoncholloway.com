## Submission URLs / requirements (researched 2026-08-01)

### Publishers Weekly
- Guidelines: https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/corp/submissionguidelines.html  
- GalleyTracker: https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/pubreview/submit/index.html  
- FAQ: https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/pubreview/faq/index.html  
- **$25 processing fee** per title · DRM-free EPUB/PDF · preferably + 2 physical galleys  
- Self / single-author: FAQ steers to **BookLife** rather than GalleyTracker for multi-house publishers

### Booklist (ALA)
- https://www.booklistonline.com/get-reviewed  
- Adult digital: email PDF + metadata to **booklistadultbooks@ala.org**  
- Fields: title, author, imprint, pub date, pages, price, format, ISBN, ebook ISBN/price, description, author bio  
- Prefer 4–6 months pre-pub

### Library Journal
- https://www.libraryjournal.com/page/Review-Submissions  
- Digital or bound galley; national-interest threshold; submission ≠ guaranteed review  
- Prefer well ahead of pub; post-pub generally avoided

### Shelf Awareness
- https://www.shelf-awareness.com/submissionguidelines.html  
- Adult digital: **galleys@shelf-awareness.com** with Title / Author / ISBN / Pub Date / genre / brief synopsis  
- Print: 2107 Elliott Ave, Suite 205, Seattle, WA 98121  
- **Currently do not review ebook-only or print-on-demand titles** — flag before any send
