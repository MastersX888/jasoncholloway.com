## What this series is

Five research explainers for the **Masters X Trilogy**. Each episode starts from a real document, measurement, or place, walks into the research, and **labels where fiction begins**.

**Register:** Desk documentary. Static camera. Physical props. Author's voice. No thumbnail-scream energy.

**Standard close:**

> The facts are in the files. The fiction is in the books. The encyclopedia holds the seam.
