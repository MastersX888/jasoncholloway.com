## THE PANEL

- **VOICE 1 — Designer** (Knopf/FSG-caliber cover designer): composition, restraint, literary-shelf register.
- **VOICE 2 — Retail** (genre/retail strategist): thumbnail survival, shelf differentiation, browser psychology, comp positioning.

**Panel ground rules established at open:**
1. *Designer:* "The jacket is the church door; the case is the crypt. Every jacket must survive a 120px thumbnail; every case is allowed to whisper — it's a reward, not an ad."
2. *Symbolist:* "Nothing on these covers that a monk's compass or a physicist's bench couldn't have produced. That's the book's whole thesis: same figure, two vocabularies."
3. *Retail:* "The three jackets must read as one series at six feet and three different books at two feet. One radial gold device per book, differentiated by *geometry*, not by clutter."
4. *Production:* "Hairline gold on K-100 black holds at 300dpi down to ~0.35pt line weight; below that it fills or vanishes. Every concept below is scored against that."

# BOOK 1 — *THE INHERITANCE OF FREQUENCY*

**Story core (from the read):** Bohemia 1267 — Brother Aldric completes the 712th figure ("a circle inscribed within a circle, bisected by lines that met at angles his mathematics could not name," p.10), burns the original as the figures rotate in the flames (p.13), scatters seven satchels. 758 years later: Box 1847, seven black-leather notebooks, the U-2 photograph ("First flight. 1956. I saw things from up there. –W," p.42), the Fibonacci-position stone lock beneath SubTropolis (p.105), the Strahov chamber behind a brass key (p.147–148), Nadia's hand found in the dark ("Human geometry. The simplest pattern. The oldest," p.107), and the epilogue dive to Site Echo — the carved stone sphere on its limestone plinth under black-glass water (p.187–191).

**Chapter frequencies:** 109 Hz · Kansas City / 111.2 Hz · Prague / 107.3 Hz · Coda.

## Jacket concepts

### B1-J1 — "The 712th Figure" (Seven-Fold Nota, mid-rotation) — *evolves the existing brief*
- **One-line visual:** A single compass-drawn seven-fold rosette in gold on black — circles within circles with visible hairline construction lines — caught *mid-rotation*: one ring a few degrees out of true, as if the figure is turning through a dimension the page can't hold.
- **Manuscript evidence:** Aldric's final figure, "the last of seven hundred and twelve. A circle inscribed within a circle, bisected by lines that met at angles his mathematics could not name… the angular pattern seemed to rotate through dimensions the candlelight couldn't explain" (p.10); the burning original whose "circles spun" (p.13); the same figure recurring in William's notebooks (p.41) and the SubTropolis carvings (p.102–103).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** Direct child of the omnibus Master Figura — but where the omnibus figure is *resolved* (all seven traditions unified), Book 1's is *unresolved*: the rotation misalignment says "beginning, not culmination." Same gold, same compass language, deliberately imperfect registration.
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "A-type mandala already won the omnibus A/B test at 100–160px. The rotation offset must be gross enough (5–7°) to survive as 'something is turning' rather than 'printing error.'" *Production:* keep the offset ring at ≥0.5pt so the moiré effect doesn't fill at 300dpi.

### B1-J2 — "The Higher I Flew" (U-2 over patterned earth)
- **One-line visual:** A thin gold arc of the curved earth at the panel's foot, one tiny U-2 silhouette above it at 70,000 feet, and between them cloud formations that resolve — as the eye descends — into Aldric's geometric figures.
- **Manuscript evidence:** William's letter: "I saw them first from seventy thousand feet. U-2 reconnaissance, 1956… Patterns in cloud formations that matched mathematical sequences I'd seen in medieval manuscripts… The higher I flew, the more I could see" (p.44); the vault photograph, "the spy plane that flew at seventy thousand feet, so high the curvature of the earth became visible" (p.42); the Cessna lesson, "There's no chaos in nature, Blake. Only patterns we don't understand yet" (p.20).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** Breaks the pure-mandala formula while keeping gold-line-on-black; smuggles the Cold War inheritance thread the omnibus couldn't foreground. The clouds-becoming-notae is the book's own move (scripture and reconnaissance photography agreeing).
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "The arc + single aircraft reads instantly and unusually — nothing on the speculative-literary shelf looks like this. Risk: reads 'aviation history' to a cold browser; the eyebrow MASTERS X and the geometric clouds must do heavy lifting." *Designer:* strongest *flap-to-flap* concept — the arc can continue across the spine to the back panel.

### B1-J3 — "Box 1847" (the reliquary still-life)
- **One-line visual:** Seven black-leather notebooks in a tight vertical stack rendered as gold engraving, the top one open to a nota; beside them the brass Oscar-01 tag and a red-wax-sealed envelope — a safe-deposit box's contents laid out like a saint's relics.
- **Manuscript evidence:** "Seven notebooks. Black leather binding, aged to a deep brown… geometric figures. Circles inscribed within circles" (p.40–41); the brass tag "CR-60 / 68093… Oscar-01" (p.41); "For Blake, when he's ready. Not before" in red wax (p.41); Box 1847 itself, "rental paid through December 2025, fifty-seven years in advance" (p.39).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** The most object-literal of the family — it trades the omnibus's abstraction for the marketing hook the site already leads with (the 57-year box, the seven notebooks). Gold engraving style keeps it in-register.
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "Still-lifes die at thumbnail unless the silhouette is bold; a stack of books reads as 'a stack' at 120px, which is fine but not magnetic." *Symbolist:* "Also the weakest theologically — the box is the inciting device, not the meaning."

## Case concepts

### B1-C1 — "Candlelight Through Vellum"
- **One-line visual:** A single translucent vellum leaf glowing amber from behind, its geometric figures showing through like stained glass, edges falling to true black — the light source implied, never shown.
- **Manuscript evidence:** "The parchment was calf vellum, scraped so thin that candlelight passed through it like stained glass. Forty calves had died for this manuscript. Aldric had blessed each one" (p.10); the trilogy's first and final line, "Restless candlelight guttered" (p.10 and p.732).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** Where the omnibus case is anatomical (Cathedral Within), Book 1's case is *material* — the manuscript itself as a body light passes through. Same warm-dark register, zero overlap.
- **Thumbnail note:** Case laminates don't need thumbnail performance; they need touch-distance reward. *Production:* the glow gradient is the one non-line-art element in the set — build it as a CMYK amber ramp (no white ink available), keep the darkest 15% pure K so the board edges disappear against the jacket interior.

### B1-C2 — "Site Echo" (the withheld room)
- **One-line visual:** A pale cone of dive-light through green-black water striking a carved stone sphere on its limestone plinth — rendered in gold-and-verdigris line art, most of the board pure black.
- **Manuscript evidence:** The epilogue dive: "his dive light cut a pale cone through the murk" (p.188); "in the center of the room, resting on a natural limestone plinth… was a sphere… the deepest carvings worn almost smooth… the sphere's faint luminescence — was it luminescent? had it always been?" (p.189–191); "waiting in the geological patience of a thing that had already waited longer than human language had existed" (p.196).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** The concept-rhyme the panel loved: *the secret under the jacket is the secret under the lake.* The reader who strips the jacket finds the thing Blake eventually renounces — the book's withheld sublime, hidden exactly where a hidden thing should be.
- **Thumbnail note:** N/A for retail; at hand distance the single lit object on a black field is the most dramatic image in the whole program. *Production:* the cone gradient needs the same amber-ramp treatment as C1; sphere carving lines at 0.4pt minimum.

## PANEL RECOMMENDATION — BOOK 1
**Jacket: B1-J1 "The 712th Figure" · Case: B1-C1 "Candlelight Through Vellum"**

*Rationale:* The rosette is the proven thumbnail winner and the series anchor — Book 1 must establish the radial gold device the other two volumes will vary. The mid-rotation imperfection gives it a Book-1-specific story (the figure is waking up) without breaking family resemblance. Vellum wins the case slot over Site Echo on *sequence* grounds — Symbolist: "The 1267 scriptorium is Book 1's own frame; the sphere belongs to the epilogue and is stronger held in reserve" — with Site Echo formally reserved as the strongest candidate for Book 1 *endpapers* or a special-edition treatment. Dissent noted: Retail preferred "The Higher I Flew" for shelf differentiation; the panel judged it the best *back-panel* art instead (the earth-arc wrapping the spine).

# BOOK 2 — *THE GRIMOIRE*

**Story core (from the read):** The scholar's odyssey. The 23-title reading list "Lectiones ante introitum" in five-century iron-gall brown (p.211), the 23 chained desks; the whiteboard where the Ars Notoria notae decode as acoustic schematics — "A wavefront diagram… Michael was not an angel. Michael was a frequency designation" (p.242); Chartres, where "the builders had put the earth's heartbeat in the floor" (p.296); Reims' coronation choir at 111.8 Hz — "Not divine right. Divine frequency" (p.300); the Specchi mirror guild of Murano, *speculari ad resonantiam* (p.406); Ghana's seven laterite caves forming a heptatonic scale (p.377–378) and Ama Ata's still hands receiving Blake's tremor (p.383–384); the eleven-minute 444.8 Hz coherence event — the "garment of glory" and the Fall reread as harmonic fall (p.391–394); and the coda: the Breitling stopping of its own accord at midnight, set on the closed ninth Moleskine — "The grimoire was sealed" (p.498–500).

**Chapter frequencies:** 3.915 Hz · Iceland / 111.2 Hz · Chartres·Prague / 111.5 Hz · Ghana / 444.8 Hz · Iceland.

## Jacket concepts

### B2-J1 — "The Michael Frequency" (Wavefront Nota) — *evolves the existing brief*
- **One-line visual:** One large Ars Notoria nota drawn as Blake drew it on the whiteboard — outer circle, inner node, four perpendicular reflection paths, Latin radiating "like the spokes of a wheel" — with small engineering-grade Hz annotations (222 · 333 · 333.6 · 444.8) where the angel-names sit.
- **Manuscript evidence:** "He drew the first nota… the figure associated with the angel Michael. A circle within a circle, bisected by two perpendicular lines, Latin words radiating outward from the center like the spokes of a wheel… A wavefront diagram. The outer circle: the boundary of the standing wave… Michael was not an angel. Michael was a frequency designation" (p.242); Andrew reading the finished whiteboard as "a circuit schematic. Angels as resistors. Harmonics as current" (p.251).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** Sibling of the omnibus figura but with the *second vocabulary* made explicit: the omnibus is pure monk; Book 2 is monk-annotated-by-physicist. The dual type system (Cinzel Latin + small mono Hz labels) enacts the book's thesis typographically.
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "Circle-with-cross-spokes silhouette is clean at 120px; the annotations vanish, which is correct — they're the touch-distance reward." *Production:* mono annotations no smaller than 6.5pt on the printed jacket or they plug.

### B2-J2 — "The Labyrinth Heartbeat" (Chartres, continued lineage)
- **One-line visual:** The eleven-circuit Chartres labyrinth in gold, its six-petal center rosette emitting faint concentric standing-wave rings that pass *through* the path — floor plan and interference pattern in one figure, with a small "3.9 Hz" mark at center.
- **Manuscript evidence:** "The labyrinth produced a different frequency from the nave. 3.9 Hz… The builders had put the earth's heartbeat in the floor. Not metaphorically. Physically… The pilgrims who walked the labyrinth were walking on the earth's fundamental frequency. They were not leaving the earth. They were entering it" (p.295–296); Blake lying on the rosette center feeling it "through every vertebra" (p.296).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** Direct evolution of the *existing* Book 2 jacket (gold Chartres-style labyrinth), so it preserves lineage recognition for early readers while upgrading it with the wave-interference overlay only this manuscript can justify.
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "Labyrinths thumbnail beautifully — dense, circular, unmistakable. Slight risk of 'mindfulness shelf' misread; the wave rings and Hz mark are the counter-signal." *Production:* eleven circuits at jacket scale = ~1pt path walls; safe.

### B2-J3 — "Speculari ad Resonantiam" (the dark mirror)
- **One-line visual:** A single parabolic Specchi mirror on a stand in a dark stone room, gold engraving; the mirror's face reflects no scene — only faint concentric wavefronts converging on its focal point.
- **Manuscript evidence:** The Venice letters "specified mirrors… speculari ad resonantiam. Mirrors for resonance" (p.406); parabolic curvatures, *politura ad quintam*, the ledger's *lumen invisibile* (p.407–408); the 1843 hand-delivery to a Jesuit mission in Kansas City (p.407); and the chamber mirrors "reflecting darkness… what mirrors in a dark room always reflect: the potential to reflect" (p.395).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** Introduces the licensed silver secondary accent (BRAND_SOURCE §6.5 — the Book 3 astrolabe grey and the silver discs) as tarnished amalgam; the only concept in the program with an *object in space*, which gives Book 2 a different depth register from Books 1 and 3.
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "A mirror that reflects rings is intriguing at full size and ambiguous at 120px — could read as a satellite dish." *Designer:* "Which is almost the point, but 'almost' loses sales." Scored third.

## Case concepts

### B2-C1 — "The Trembling Line"
- **One-line visual:** One gold ruled line crossing the entire board at title-underline height — steady, then breaking into a precise 111.2 Hz sine wobble, then almost settling — with "111.2 Hz" set small beneath the disturbance.
- **Manuscript evidence:** "He set down the pen and watched the tremor put a wobble into the final period" (p.208); "the tremor-script, the seismographic notation that was itself a frequency record, each wobble a data point, each letter an artifact of the 111.2 Hz that his nervous system had been broadcasting for eight years" (p.494); the pen "whose nib had been shaped by his tremor into a writing instrument that could not be used by anyone whose hand did not vibrate at 111.2 Hz" (p.717).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** The boldest, most ownable mark in the whole program — Concept B from the omnibus process, judged "too quiet for retail" there and therefore *perfect* here, hidden under the jacket where quiet is a virtue. Book 2 is the tremor's book; this is its autograph.
- **Thumbnail note:** N/A retail. *Production:* dead simple — one path, foil-friendly, and the strongest candidate in the program for a *blind deboss + gold foil* combination on the case stock.

### B2-C2 — "Midnight, Sealed" (Breitling on the Moleskine)
- **One-line visual:** The stopped Breitling Navitimer, hands at 12:00, resting on the closed, swollen ninth Moleskine — engraved gold-and-silver line art, drawn like a plate from an instrument catalog, captioned in small Latin-style caps: *THE GRIMOIRE WAS SEALED.*
- **Manuscript evidence:** "The Breitling stopped. Not wound down, the mainspring had thirty-two hours of power reserve… The hour hand, at 12:00, precise, centered, frozen" (p.498–499); "He set the closed Moleskine on the desk. Set the stopped Breitling on top of it. The grimoire was sealed" (p.500); the silence "louder than its ticking had ever been… the way the silence after a bell is the bell's truest note" (p.499).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** The trilogy's beating-heart prop given its one sanctioned appearance — the panel agreed the watch must appear exactly once in the program, and this tableau is the book's own closing ritual.
- **Thumbnail note:** N/A retail. *Production:* watch-face detail wants 0.35–0.5pt lines; render as engraving, never photographic, or it reads as an ad for Breitling.

## PANEL RECOMMENDATION — BOOK 2
**Jacket: B2-J1 "The Michael Frequency" · Case: B2-C2 "Midnight, Sealed"**

*Rationale:* The Wavefront Nota is the trilogy's central visual pun (medieval diagram = acoustics schematic) and it belongs on the volume actually titled *The Grimoire*; the Hz-annotated angel-figure is an image no other book on the shelf can legally own. The Labyrinth was a close second and is preserved as the back-panel device (it also honors the outgoing jacket lineage). For the case, the panel *overturned the existing brief* (which had the Trembling Line here): the trembling line is promoted to the **cross-spine set device** (see SPINES below), where it does more work, and the case slot goes to "Midnight, Sealed" — a figurative, ritual, end-of-book image that rewards the finished reader rather than the browser. Ghana's laterite amber enters the program as Book 2's accent temperature (warmer gold than Books 1/3, per the "amber… not a color, a density" passage, p.375) rather than as a separate concept.

# BOOK 3 — *THE KINGDOM*

**Story core (from the read):** Return and distribution. Twelve chairs in a circle in fluorescent Room 204; Mara Reeves: "It sounds like being inside a cathedral… The dome is my skull. The walls are my ribs. I'm the building" (p.517); Blake's whiteboard — nave cross-section beside human torso, 1.618 : 1.62 (p.518); the accelerometer catching a 108 Hz standing wave from twelve breathing students (p.520). The ninth page: "Two figures, seated, facing each other. The concentric nota rings, open, unenclosed" — Nadia: "This isn't an instruction. This is our marriage" (p.565). Nine hundred listening groups; "The kingdom was a grandmother in a folding chair. It had always been" (p.714). Nadia leaves at 5:12 AM, convent ring back on, the crossed-out name ("The absence was the note," p.671); the house drops to 53.6 Hz, "one heartbeat where there used to be two" (p.679). Her 5 AM discovery: 107.8 Hz, "Here is what I heard: everything" (p.703–705). Two notebooks on the kitchen table, "Teach me" (p.720–721). The final chapter: cream paper, the Breitling left to run down unwound, Aldric's gesture across 775 years, "Restless candlelight guttered" (p.725–732).

**Chapter frequencies:** 109 → 111.2 → 67 (departure) → 53.6 (empty kitchen) → 107.8 (hers) → 107.3 (his).

## Jacket concepts

### B3-J1 — "The Cathedral Within" (per existing brief — flagged)
- **One-line visual:** Gothic nave cross-section ghosted into a human torso, engraved gold line art — the whiteboard drawing made cover.
- **Manuscript evidence:** "He drew two diagrams side by side: a cross-section of a Gothic cathedral nave, and a cross-section of a human torso. The same proportions… 1.618… 1.62. A discrepancy of 0.002" (p.518); Mara: "The dome is my skull. The walls are my ribs. I'm the building" (p.517).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** **Here the panel overturns the existing brief.** This is, nearly stroke for stroke, the omnibus *case laminate* (Cathedral Within). Putting it on Book 3's retail jacket makes the trilogy volume disclose the omnibus's hidden reward — Designer: "You'd be selling the crypt on the church door of a different church." Viable only if Jason wants deliberate cross-format echo; the panel recommends against.
- **Thumbnail note:** Proven collapse at retail size — the omnibus A/B test showed Concept D "collapses to noise" at 100–160px. Double disqualification.

### B3-J2 — "The Ninth Page" (the marriage diagram)
- **One-line visual:** Two small seated figures facing each other in profile, compass-abstracted almost to notae themselves, inside concentric gold rings that are *open* — the outermost rings breaking and spreading off the panel edges, unenclosed.
- **Manuscript evidence:** The ninth page itself: "Two figures, seated, facing each other. The concentric nota rings, open, unenclosed. No mirrors. No cave. No preparation apparatus" (p.565); Nadia: "This isn't an instruction. This is our marriage… The gap. The difference between the person who goes into the cave and the person who stays at the entrance" (p.565–566); the rings "spreading into the kitchen and the house and the foundation beneath the house… and the frequency of the earth beneath everything" (p.568–569).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** Completes the family arc as an argument: B1 = one figure waking (rotation), B2 = the figure as instrument (annotation), B3 = the figure *opened* and inhabited by two people. The broken outer rings are the visual form of the book's thesis — distribution, unenclosure, "you cannot un-release a resonance."
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "Radial silhouette survives; the two figures must be sized so they read as 'two people' and not 'smudge' at 120px — target ≥18% of panel height. The open/broken rings are legible even tiny because the eye expects closure and doesn't get it." *Production:* figures as filled gold silhouettes (not outlines) for thumbnail mass.

### B3-J3 — "The Circle of Twelve" (chairs as rose window)
- **One-line visual:** Thirteen folding chairs seen in plan view from directly above, arranged in a circle on black — at distance the composition reads as a gold rosette/rose window; up close it's just chairs, one of them subtly different (the thirteenth).
- **Manuscript evidence:** "Twelve chairs arranged in a circle by Marcus… Blake entered without introduction. Sat in the thirteenth chair. The circle closed" (p.515–516); the 108 Hz standing wave from twelve breathing bodies (p.520); "The kingdom was a grandmother in a folding chair. It had always been" (p.714); Nadia's data — "folding chairs match twelve weeks of the 52-week protocol."
- **Conversation with omnibus:** The wittiest concept in the program: the omnibus's sacred rose-window geometry rebuilt from the humblest possible objects — which is precisely Book 3's reversal (cathedral → classroom → kitchen). The site's rose-window background becomes a circle of chairs and nobody notices until they do.
- **Thumbnail note:** *Retail:* "At 120px it IS a mandala — total series coherence — and the double-take at full size is a bookseller's talking point." *Production:* chairs in plan view are simple orthogonal line clusters; very safe at 300dpi.

## Case concepts

- **One-line visual:** Two gold waveforms crossing the board — one labeled 107.3 Hz, one 107.8 Hz — producing between them a visible interference pattern (the beat), with the composite wave continuing alone to the board's edge.
- **Manuscript evidence:** Nadia's fourteenth entry: "Every room where two people sit together produces a composite wave, the shape made by two signals crossing" (p.704); her own frequency, "107.8 Hz… Not Blake's frequency. Not the cohort's. Hers" (p.703); his settled 107.3, "the sound of Blake Masters' body at rest" (p.721); the negative image — the house at 53.6 Hz, "one heartbeat where there used to be two" (p.679).

- **Thumbnail note:** N/A retail. *Production:* two 0.75pt paths plus a fine interference lattice; the beat-frequency envelope must be mathematically real (the panel insists — the readers this rewards will check).

### B3-C2 — "Two Gospels" (the kitchen table)
- **One-line visual:** A kitchen table seen from above in gold line art: two closed notebooks (a worn Moleskine, a leather Prague notebook), two coffee cups, and a single band of morning light crossing the table from the salt shaker toward the sugar bowl.
- **Manuscript evidence:** "On the table, between the coffee cups: two notebooks… Two gospels. His ended with the Word. Hers ended with the hearing" (p.720); the recurring domestic sundial — "the morning light crosses from the salt shaker to the sugar bowl over the course of one cup of coffee" (p.508, 563, 715); "Teach me" (p.721).
- **Conversation with omnibus:** The trilogy's actual destination — not the sphere, not the chamber, a table. As the innermost printed surface of the final volume, it is the last image the design program ever shows the reader, and it is the quietest. The panel considers that correct.
- **Thumbnail note:** N/A retail. *Production:* the light band is the one gradient; same amber-ramp build as B1's vellum glow, keeping the family's material logic (light = amber ramp, everything else = gold line).

## PANEL RECOMMENDATION — BOOK 3
**Jacket: B3-J2 "The Ninth Page" · Case: B3-C2 "Two Gospels"**

"The Circle of Twelve" is preserved as the recommended B3 *back panel* (and a strong future paperback-conversion front). "Two Frequencies" is recommended for B3 endpapers, where the 107.3/107.8 joke can live at full width.

# THE SPINES AS A SET

Three devices, one system, per the panel:

1. **The Trembling Rule (new — the set's signature).** A single gold horizontal rule crossing all three spines at the same height. On Book 1 it runs *straight* and just begins to disturb at the trailing edge (the gift waking, 109 Hz). On Book 2 it breaks into the full **111.2 Hz** wobble across the entire spine (the tremor's book). On Book 3 it *settles* — wobble decaying to a calm 107.3 Hz ripple by the tail (the frequency of not looking; his body's chosen note). Shelved in order, the three spines draw the trilogy's whole arc as one trembling line — the tremor-script motif (p.208, 494) made furniture. Shelved out of order it still reads as a family rule.
2. **Frequency-and-place notation** (the book's own chapter-header system) set small in mono under each title: `109 Hz · KANSAS CITY` / `111.2 Hz · PRAGUE·ICELAND` / `107.3 Hz · KANSAS CITY`. Canon, not decoration (MANUSCRIPT_ANALYSIS §5.1).
3. **Constants:** Cinzel wide-tracked title cream, volume numeral gold, heptagram imprint at every spine foot, K-100 ground. The three jacket hero devices do **not** repeat on the spines — the rule and the Hz do the set work; the spines stay quiet and inscriptional, per the literary-shelf register.

*Production:* the rule aligns across books only if set as a fixed distance from the **head** trim (spine widths differ: ~0.87" / ~1.26" / ~0.95", to be re-derived per Ingram templates before export — do not reuse archive spine values). Wobble amplitude ≤ 25% of the narrowest spine width so Book 1 never clips.
