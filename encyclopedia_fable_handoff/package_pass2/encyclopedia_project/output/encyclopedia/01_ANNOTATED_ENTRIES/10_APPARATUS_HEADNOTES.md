## HEADNOTE — THE SOLFEGGIO FREQUENCIES

The nine-tone Solfeggio table (174–963 Hz, with the six syllable-named core tones UT 396 · RE 417 · MI 528 · FA 639 · SOL 741 · LA 852) reproduces a real modern wellness tradition — and the encyclopedia's obligation is to date it honestly. The "ancient Solfeggio scale" is a late-twentieth-century construction: the syllable names descend from Guido of Arezzo's genuine eleventh-century solmization of the hymn *Ut queant laxis*, but the specific frequency assignments and their healing associations ("DNA repair," "liberation from fear") originate in 1990s numerological derivation from Numbers 7, not in any medieval tuning practice. No peer-reviewed evidence supports frequency-specific healing claims. The trilogy knows this and uses the tradition accordingly — as one of several folk frequency-maps that the Masters system quietly declines to endorse while borrowing their cultural resonance. The table is printed because readers will arrive with these numbers already in their heads; the headnote is printed so they leave knowing which century the numbers come from. *(Associations as tabulated: `frequency_data/frequency_bands.json`, `solfeggio_frequencies`.)*

## HEADNOTE — THE LLOYD HEALING CODES

Dr. Alexander Loyd's Healing Codes system directs hand positions at four bodily "healing centers" — bridge (between the eyebrows), jaw, temples, and Adam's apple — which the trilogy's data file maps onto frequency ranges (bridge 110–140 Hz; jaw 140–280; temples 280–528; Adam's apple 528–963: `frequency_data/frequency_bands.json`, `lloyd_healing_codes`). Two honesty notes govern this table. First, the frequency-range mapping is the *novel's* synthesis, not Loyd's — the published Healing Codes system does not assign Hz values; the JSON's `_theory` line marks the correspondence as constructed. Second, the system itself is a commercial self-help practice without controlled-trial support. The trilogy's interest is structural, not clinical: a modern practice that assigns *locations on the body* to *categories of repair* rhymes with the Ars Notoria's body-as-instrument diagrams, and it is that rhyme — not any therapeutic claim — that earns the table its page. Note the fiction's fingerprint: the bridge center's range begins at 110 Hz, the archaeoacoustic band on which the entire Masters system is built. *(Orthography: the data file spells "Lloyd"; the published author of* The Healing Code *spells his name Alex(ander) Loyd — reconcile at the print pass.)*

## HEADNOTE — THE QUANTUM HEALING CODES

The Grabovoi-style number sequences tabulated here (e.g., 9187948181 for "complete health restoration," with the file deriving pseudo-frequency analogs such as 918.7 Hz from the digit strings: `frequency_data/frequency_bands.json`, `quantum_healing_codes`) belong to a living internet numerology whose founder, Grigori Grabovoi, was convicted of fraud in Russia in 2008 after claiming, among other things, the ability to resurrect the dead. The encyclopedia prints the table for one reason: the trilogy is a study of what people do with the *idea* that number and reality are coupled, and no contemporary tradition demonstrates that idea's appeal — or its dangers — more nakedly. The "hz_analog" column is the data file's own invention (digits recast as decimals); it corresponds to no physical measurement. Readers should treat this table as an exhibit in the trilogy's museum of frequency belief, shelved deliberately next to the Completion Sect.

## HEADNOTE — THE CHAKRA FREQUENCIES

The chakra table maps the seven-center subtle-body schema of Indian tantric traditions onto Hz values (`frequency_data/frequency_bands.json`, `chakra_frequencies`). The centers themselves are a genuine and ancient contemplative anatomy, attested in Sanskrit sources from roughly the first millennium CE; the *frequency assignments* are modern Western syncretism with no basis in the source traditions, which described the chakras in terms of petals, syllables, and deities — not Hertz. The trilogy's comparative thesis (attunement traditions arise independently across cultures — cf. the Ama Ata Asante entry and the Osage *Wah-kon-tah* layer in `research_synthesis/INDEPENDENCE_ZION_THEOLOGICAL_INTEGRATION.md`) is served by the real half of this table: a second, fully independent tradition locating perception in prepared bodily centers. The numeric half is printed in the spirit of the Solfeggio headnote — documented as belief, not as measurement.
