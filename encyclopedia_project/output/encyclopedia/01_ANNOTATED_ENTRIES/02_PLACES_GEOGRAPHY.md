## KANSAS CITY & QUALITY HILL

**In the novels.** The trilogy's ground zero — the city Blake's opening chapter meets at "109 Hz · Kansas City" and the site of the Foundation's Quality Hill entrance, where the first cohort's twenty-three candidates are photographed on graduation day (*Omnibus*, Vol. III). The narrative claim: the city sits over sacred, resonant ground, and the trilogy's factions have contested it since 1924.

**In the world.** Quality Hill is a real bluff neighborhood — roughly 200 feet above the West Bottoms, "the highest ground in Kansas City" — crowned by the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception: **red brick**, Renaissance Revival, built 1882–1912, gold-leaf cupola added 1960 (`research_synthesis/KC_CHURCHES_RESEARCH_ANALYSIS.md`, Correction #1 — the research pass specifically corrected an earlier limestone description). The deeper claim of significance is the encyclopedia's Part Three thesis: at least five independent traditions — Hopewell mound builders (100 BCE–700 CE, ~30 sites between Sibley, MO and Atchison, KS), the Osage *Wah-kon-tah* corridor, Joseph Smith's 1831 Zion revelation, the Community of Christ's Fibonacci-spiral temple (1994), and Roger Billings' 1980 relocation — converge on this geography (`research_synthesis/INDEPENDENCE_ZION_THEOLOGICAL_INTEGRATION.md`, thesis section).

**Cross-references:** Independence & the Temple Lot · SubTropolis · The West Bottoms · The Keepers (KC since 1924).

## SUBTROPOLIS & THE MOREAU CHAMBER

**In the novels.** Blake's former workplace (the struck-through G4S badge) and, later, the Foundation's deep infrastructure: "160 feet below, in the SubTropolis limestone, in the converted Moreau church basement that had become the Foundation's primary research chamber, the mirrors waited. Six parabolic surfaces, each ground to the Specchi specification" (*Omnibus*, Vol. III). Depth is canon-locked at **160 feet** (`canon/CANON.md` §3). Post-demonstration, "the seventy-two-hour resonance still decaying in the geological substrate."

**In the world.** SubTropolis is real — the world's largest underground commercial complex, excavated from the **Bethany Falls Limestone** formation in Kansas City (`universe_memory/02_FACT_BEHIND_FICTION.md`, KC Geography table; `KC_Churches_Research/SubTropolis/` in the catalog). The same formation hosts Roger Billings' 180-acre underground compound near Independence — laboratories, workshops, living quarters (`research_synthesis/ROGER_BILLINGS_MASTERS_X_ANALYSIS.md`). A converted-church-basement chamber inside it is fiction; the geology is not.

**Cross-references:** The Specchi Mirrors · Masters Foundation · Moreau, Father · Independence & the Temple Lot · 160 feet.

## MILLER NICHOLS LIBRARY

**In the novels.** UMKC's library; "the corner table… had been Blake Masters' territory for three years" (*Omnibus*, Vol. I ch. 1, "The Library Boy," keyed 109 Hz). Repository of the **Moreau collection** — manuscripts "hidden in plain sight where Blake had spent five years of graduate school and never known to look" — visited by William Masters in 1967, 1972, and 1985.

**In the world.** Miller Nichols Library is the real UMKC library (General Library **1969**; renamed **1991** — [Clio](https://theclio.com/entry/141768)). No Moreau collection exists `[fiction]`. Atmospheric use per omnibus copyright disclaimer.

**Cross-references:** Moreau, Father · Masters, William · Masters, Blake.

## HOTEL PHILLIPS BUILDING

**In the novels.** Nadia's institutional seat — "The Hotel Phillips Building office in November. Third cup of coffee, black with one sugar, stirred" (*Omnibus*, Vol. II). In Book 3 the Iceland/Kansas City axis is drawn between "the stone cottage and the Hotel Phillips Building office."

**In the world.** The Hotel Phillips is a real **1931** downtown KC landmark (Boillot & Lauck; NRHP **1979**) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Phillips). Used atmospherically per the omnibus disclaimer.

**Cross-references:** Volkov, Nadia · The Masters Foundation · Kansas City & Quality Hill.

## THE WEST BOTTOMS

**In the novels.** The industrial flats where the trilogy keeps its working spaces — the Keepers' warehouse archive where Blake and Nadia work "close enough that their elbows occasionally touched," the wine bar with Missouri wines chalked by the glass, and, below, the Moreau chamber "beneath the industrial memory of the West Bottoms" (*Omnibus*, Vols. I–III). Site of Nadia's Jesuit-vineyards aside: "Missouri was the third-largest wine-producing state before Prohibition. The Jesuits planted the first vineyards in the 1820s."

**In the world.** The West Bottoms is Kansas City's historic industrial district beneath Quality Hill. **Historical note:** Nadia's "third-largest wine-producing **state**" line is in-novel dialogue; documented pre-Prohibition ranking is **second-largest US state** (Stone Hill Winery among largest wineries worldwide) — [Wikipedia: Missouri wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_wine). Jesuit viticulture frame: `research_synthesis/JESUIT_BOOKS_USAGE_GUIDE.md`.

## STRAHOV MONASTERY & CRYPT (PRAGUE)

**In the novels.** The trilogy's European keystone, reached in Vol. I chs. 13–15 ("111.2 Hz · Prague"). Before dawn, with "a brass key and the cost of three generations," Miroslav Khoury — who lives above a bookshop on Nerudova Street — leads Blake and Nadia through Pohořelec to the library. The crypt beneath holds "what Rudolf II spent his life gathering"; William saw it in 1965, sealed it, and chose silence. Blake's first exposure here sets his permanent 111.2 Hz tremor. Eva Černá's Strahov paper later drags the site into the scholarly record.

**In the world.** Strahov is a real Premonstratensian monastery above Malá Strana; Nerudova and Pohořelec are real streets; Rudolf II's Prague really was Europe's alchemical court, gathering Dee, Kelley, and the manuscript trade (`Rudolf II and Prague`, `The magic circle of Rudolf II`, `The Enochian magick of Dr. John Dee` — see `universe_memory/02_FACT_BEHIND_FICTION.md`, Medieval/Esoteric table). The crypt chamber and its contents are fiction.

**Cross-references:** 111.2 Hz · The Strahov Cymatics Discs · Černá, Eva · Aldric, Brother · Masters, William.

## VOLTA REGION CAVES (GHANA)

**In the novels.** Nadia's Book 3 territory (`canon/CANON.md` §3) and Kofi Asante's dataset: seven mapped caves near Ho; six produce standing waves within 2% of the Strahov specification; the seventh produces an unexplained secondary resonance that appears only under specific conditions (*Omnibus*, Vol. III). Used for Ashanti funeral drumming for eight centuries; Blake's arrival by road ("a curve that Blake would have classified as suicidal and Kofi classified as Tuesday") opens the trilogy's most sustained argument that the phenomenon is global and old.

**In the world.** Ghana's Volta Region and Ashanti/Ewe drumming traditions are real. **The specific cave acoustics and Strahov-frequency matches are fiction** — label on entry face. Comparative frame: `research_synthesis/INDEPENDENCE_ZION_THEOLOGICAL_INTEGRATION.md`.

**Cross-references:** Asante, Dr. Kofi · Asante, Ama Ata · Volkov, Nadia · The Frequency Band Ladder.

## INDEPENDENCE & THE TEMPLE LOT

**In the novels.** The theological shadow under the trilogy's geography. **No direct Temple Lot scene** in v8 (grep-verified; see `output/VERIFICATION_LOG.md`). The novels approach Independence obliquely through the Keepers' 1924 founding and the limestone continuum.

**In the world.** In July 1831 Joseph Smith declared Independence, Missouri the "center place" of Zion (D&C 57) — New Jerusalem site, temple site, and by LDS tradition the vicinity of the Garden of Eden; the 63-acre Temple Lot was dedicated August 3, 1831, with plans drawn for 24 temples; it is maintained today by multiple Restoration denominations (`research_synthesis/INDEPENDENCE_ZION_THEOLOGICAL_INTEGRATION.md`, Layer 2). In 1980 Roger Billings relocated his operation to Independence, founded "The Church of Jesus Christ in Zion" as its "patriarch and prophet," and built a compound in 180 underground limestone acres nearby (`research_synthesis/ROGER_BILLINGS_MASTERS_X_ANALYSIS.md`). The research file's summary line: "A man who believes he is a prophet built an underground city inside the caves of the prophesied Zion."

**Cross-references:** Kansas City & Quality Hill · SubTropolis & the Moreau Chamber · Masters, William (Billings parallel).
