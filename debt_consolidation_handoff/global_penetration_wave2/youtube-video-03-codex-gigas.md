## Description (paste into YouTube Studio)

The Codex Gigas — the "Devil's Bible" — is the largest surviving medieval manuscript in the world: 92 cm tall, roughly 75 kg, famous for its full-page devil illustration. It was made in thirteenth-century Bohemia, owned by Emperor Rudolf II in Prague, and taken to Stockholm as war booty in 1648.

In this video I trace the real history of the Codex Gigas — from Benedictine scriptorium to Rudolf's court to the Swedish National Library — and why Bohemian manuscript culture sits at the center of Central European thriller fiction.

I'm Jason Carroll Holloway (Seventh City Press), author of the Masters X trilogy. The trilogy's prologue opens in Bohemia, 1267 — **fiction** built on **real** codex history. Full Field Note linked below.
