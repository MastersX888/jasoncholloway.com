## Author Central bio (UK + DE + AU — paste same EN text)

Jason Carroll Holloway is the author of the **Masters X** trilogy — a literary conspiracy thriller linking medieval manuscripts, acoustic science, and the sealed crypt beneath Prague's Strahov Library. Published by Seventh City Press.

His work sits on the shelf beside Umberto Eco, Dan Brown, and Elizabeth Kostova: real history as engine, fiction as argument. The trilogy opens in the limestone beneath Kansas City and converges on Central Europe — Voynich-adjacent codices, grimoires as cognitive technology, and a resonant frequency that restructures the nervous system.

He holds an M.A. in English Literature from Mercy University and lives in Kansas City. Author site: https://jasoncholloway.com/
