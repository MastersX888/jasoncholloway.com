## Narration register

- **Pace:** literary fiction — not thriller rush; not meditation drone. ~140–160 wpm effective.
- **Blake's prologues / reconstructions:** slightly slower, more deliberate first paragraph.
- **Action / dive sequences:** allow shorter sentences to breathe; no `[excited]` tags unless v3 test pass warrants.
- **Epigraphs:** pause before and after; speak attribution after em dash quietly.
- **Chapter frequency keys:** same tone every time — ritual marker, not announced as "chapter header."
