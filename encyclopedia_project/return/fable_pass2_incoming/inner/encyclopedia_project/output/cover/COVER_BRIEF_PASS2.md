## 2. Design system (locked brand, executed)

- **Ground:** K-100 black, matte lam.
- **Gold:** CMYK (0, .15, .60, .25) — every gold element doubles as the **foil-plate
  separation** (single spot layer at production; no gradients; ≥0.5 pt strokes).
- **Companion front:** MASTERS X eyebrow → THE UNIVERSE COMPANION (tracked Cinzel Bold) →
  *The Facts Behind the Fiction* → heptagram with construction circles/radials (the
  engineering-drawing register: apparatus, not narrative) → frequency-band rule motif →
  author → imprint. Sibling, not clone, of the trilogy jackets; no trilogy hero art reused.
- **Dictionary fronts:** matched series stack; **giant Cinzel numeral** over a dimmed
  construction heptagram; volume range + entry count; author; imprint. The three spines
  shelve as a matched run — series title, `VOL. n · RANGE`, HOLLOWAY, heptagram at foot.
- **Backs:** description copy in gold Garamond; register statement; imprint block; **white
  barcode zones** (2 × 1.2 in) with ISBN placeholders.
- **Slipcase:** side A carries the series title + heptagram construction + set line
  (`THREE VOLUMES · 115,504 ENTRIES`); closed end carries the rotated series title, volume
  list, Companion cross-reference, and the **set ISBN** barcode zone; side B carries the
  large construction mark + "The pattern beneath everything."
