# Series Bible — "The Facts Behind the Fiction"

## Register

Literary-documentary. First-person, measured, no thumbnail-scream energy. You are a writer-researcher at a desk, not a YouTube personality.

**Explicitly not:** Dan Brown energy, occult influencer, AI avatar, stock-footage montage, epic trailer music, face-reaction thumbnails.

**Comps (tone only):** PBS restraint · research-essay seriousness · "here is the document" credibility.

## Visual system (lock before shoot)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Ground | K-black mat or desk surface — not grey, not wood grain distraction |
| Key light | Warm side light (~3200K), soft; one source; no flat ring-light |
| Camera | Static tripod; 24fps or 30fps; 4K capture, deliver 1080p |
| Framing | Medium close on desk OR tight on hands/documents; face optional, not required every shot |
| On-screen text | Cinzel caps, tracked; gold on black; footer: `f = 111.2 Hz` on every text card |
| Archival inserts | B&W or desaturated; hold 3–5 sec minimum; no Ken Burns abuse |
| Grade | Slight warmth; crushed blacks; gold accent ≈ #C9A227 in graphics (not in log footage) |

## Anti-slop rules (non-negotiable)

1. **No AI talking head** — not negotiable for this series.
2. **No AI-generated B-roll** of cathedrals, monks, or caves.
3. **Physical proof beats CGI** — Chladni plate, printed pages, map pins, real books.
4. **One idea per shot** — hold longer than feels comfortable.
5. **Label the seam** — on-screen grades: `measured` / `contested` / `invented` / `scholarly`.
6. **Author photo** — use `public/media/JasonCHolloway-v2.png` only; never AI face.
