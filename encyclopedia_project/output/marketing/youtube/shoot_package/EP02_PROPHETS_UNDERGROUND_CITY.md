## Narration script (verbatim)

### HOOK (0:00–0:50)

> When people ask why a conspiracy trilogy is set in Kansas City instead of Rome or Prague — and it does get to Prague — I show them a land record. One hundred and eighty acres. Underground. Purchased by a man who founded his own church and built laboratories in the dark, inside the ground his tradition calls the New Jerusalem.

**OST:** `Jackson County, Missouri`

---

### BEAT 1 — Mound builders (0:50–2:45)

> Layer one: the Hopewell tradition, roughly 100 BCE to 700 CE, along the Missouri bluffs. About thirty sites. Stone-vault tombs entered by doorways — people were building chambers in this ground two thousand years before the novel.

**OST:** `100 BCE – 700 CE`

---

### BEAT 2 — Wah-kon-tah (2:45–4:15)

> Layer two: the Osage corridor and *Wah-kon-tah* — the sacred mystery that connects everything. An attunement tradition with no need for notation.

**OST:** `Wah-kon-tah`

---

### BEAT 3 — Zion (4:15–6:15)

> Layer three: July 1831, Doctrine and Covenants 57; Independence as the center place; the sixty-three-acre Temple Lot; a plat for twenty-four temples; the temple never built; the lot still kept by three denominations.

**OST:** `August 3, 1831 · 63 acres`

---

### BEAT 4 — The engineer (6:15–9:00)

> Layer five — I skip four in the hook because the public record is the shock: Roger Billings, hydrogen car at seventeen, the Academy, Independence in 1980. Beneath the surface: his own church, patriarch and prophet, one hundred and eighty underground acres of Bethany Falls limestone — the same stone as SubTropolis. I source this carefully: public records, reporting, his son's public statements.

**OST:** `1980 · Independence, MO`

---

### BEAT 5 — The stone (9:00–10:45)

> SubTropolis at one hundred sixty feet. The quarrying boom of 1880 to 1920. Westport Presbyterian, 2011 — everything burned except the limestone walls. The stone remembers — as documentary fact, not metaphor.

**OST:** `2011 — the walls stood`

---

### BEAT 6 — What the novel adds (10:45–11:30)

> William Masters is the fictional sixth seeker. The ethical inversion: where the real compound is fenced and surveilled, the Foundation is consent-first — and finally public-domain.

---

### CTA (11:30–12:00)

> The full five-layer essay, with the file citations, is in the encyclopedia — jasoncholloway.com.
>
> The facts are in the files. The fiction is in the books. The encyclopedia holds the seam.
